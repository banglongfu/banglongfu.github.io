## Research Papers

### Regime Switching Conditional Factor Models

With Fa Wang and Yiming Wang. Revised and resubmitted to the *Journal of Business & Economic Statistics*.

- Presented at the 18th Annual Society for Financial Econometrics (SoFiE) Conference, 2026.
- Outstanding Paper Award, National Academic Forum for Doctoral Students in Quantitative Economics, 2026.
- We develop a regime-switching extension of Instrumented PCA to capture abrupt changes in risk exposures and prices of risk. The model is estimated using an EM algorithm and applied to U.S. equity returns.

### Recovering Weak Factors in Latent Groups

With Fa Wang and Yiming Wang. Work in progress.

### From Static-Observable to Dynamic-Latent: A Paradigm Shift in Chinese Corporate Bond Pricing Based on IPCA

With Fa Wang and Yiming Wang. In Chinese.

We apply IPCA to Chinese corporate bond excess returns, propose a threshold method for estimating the number of latent factors, and develop an SVD-based algorithm for testing zero pricing errors.
